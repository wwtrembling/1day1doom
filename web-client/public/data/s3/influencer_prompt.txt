Anya, once a lifestyle influencer, now desperately tries to regain followers in the clone apocalypse. Her "lewk" is tragically outdated: a ripped neon pink tracksuit smeared with what might be nutrient paste, a selfie stick duct-taped to a rusty pipe, and a cracked, oversized "sponsored" sunglasses perched precariously on her head. Her face is a mask of perpetually strained enthusiasm, a manic grin plastered over wide, terrified eyes. She’s awkwardly filming a TikTok, the background a chaotic wasteland of discarded clone-body parts and malfunctioning, identical Anya-clones. One clone is attempting a synchronized dance routine with a detached limb, another is desperately scrolling through an ancient smartphone, and Anya’s voiceover drones, "Hey besties! Surviving the end of the world with this *amazing* DIY nutrient smoothie recipe! Link in bio for a special discount code!" A single, flickering holographic "like" appears on the screen.
