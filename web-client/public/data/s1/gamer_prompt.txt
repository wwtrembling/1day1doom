A gamer, once identifiable by their Mountain Dew-stained "Keep Calm and Game On" t-shirt, now sports a tattered, mismatched ensemble scavenged from fallen clones. One sleeve is ripped, exposing the cheap, plastic-like skin of the imposter. His once-pristine gaming headset is jury-rigged with duct tape and scavenged wires, antennae reaching haphazardly towards the sky. His eyes, bloodshot and frantic, dart between a flickering, scavenged monitor displaying a pixelated, glitching Pac-Man clone desperately trying to eat escape pods. He nervously chews on a controller joystick, now repurposed as a makeshift tooth-pick, while desperately attempting to find a Wi-Fi signal amidst a sea of vacant, identical faces wandering the wasteland. Buildings in the background are plastered with propaganda, "You are You," next to a poorly photoshopped image of a zombie smiling.
