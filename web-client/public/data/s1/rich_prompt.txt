A flabby, clone-rich man in a tattered, gold-embroidered smoking jacket (one sleeve ripped off, exposing a decaying robotic arm grafted on) desperately rummages through a dumpster overflowing with discarded caviar tins. His monocle, cracked and held together with duct tape, perpetually slides down his sweaty face. He glares at it with a mix of fury and confusion. A pristine, unblemished clone wearing the exact same jacket (but spotless), wanders past, aimlessly clicking a diamond-encrusted smartphone. The clone ignores him. In the background, a billboard advertises "Clone Concierge Services: Where you *think* you belong!". Decaying skyscrapers loom, their pristine glass facades cracked, each displaying a flickering "ERROR 404: Identity Not Found" message.
