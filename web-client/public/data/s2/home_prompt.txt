A dishevelled "home" clone, clutching a rusty mop handle like a weapon, peers out from behind a barricade of overturned, identical sofas. Their once pristine, company-issued "Clean-Bot" uniform is now ripped and stained with what appears to be both ketchup and existential dread. A crudely fashioned bandana, ripped from a floral curtain, covers their thinning, slightly-off-kilter cloned hair. Their face, a perfect replica of someone, yet utterly vacant, is smeared with dust and a single, un-wiped tear. They're cautiously peeking over the barricade, watching a horde of other "home" clones, all identical, aimlessly wandering the street. Each clone is also armed with various cleaning implements, all equally useless. In the background, a billboard reads "You're Home!" with the "You" portion of the text crudely scratched out, leaving only the word "Home!" to stand out.
