Foreground:
An office worker slumps over their desk,
holding their breath as they make a final,
painfully precise adjustment to a single bent paperclip.
Their fingers hover for a moment too long,
as if one wrong move could ruin everything.
Their face is blank, tired, and far too serious for the task.

Background:
Through the office window, a few identical, unnatural figures
stand quietly in a loose line.
They are not attacking.
They are not moving.
They seem to be waiting.

Something about the world outside is clearly wrong,
but it has been politely reduced to background noise.
